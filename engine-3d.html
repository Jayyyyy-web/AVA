STAGES & WHERE EFFICIENCY IS WON

FanMoves most air around the core — the big propulsive lever (bypass).
CompressorBooster + HP stages squeeze the air. Higher pressure ratio → more thermal efficiency.
CombustorFuel burns hot (>1500°C) — more work per kg of fuel.
TurbinesHP + LP stages extract power to drive the compressor & fan.
