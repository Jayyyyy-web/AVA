STAGES & WHERE EFFICIENCY IS WON

FanMoves most of the air around the core — the big propulsive lever (bypass).
LP booster + HP compressorMany rotor+stator stages squeeze the core air. Higher pressure ratio → better thermal efficiency.
Annular combustorFuel burns. Hotter gas (>1500°C) = more work per kg of fuel.
HP + LP turbinesExtract power to drive the compressor & fan. Materials set the heat limit.
Bypass flowCool air around the core through guide-vane struts — most of the thrust, quietly.
