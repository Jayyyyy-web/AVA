STAGES & WHERE EFFICIENCY IS WON

FanMoves most of the air around the core — the big propulsive-efficiency lever (bypass).
CompressorSqueezes the core air. Higher pressure ratio → better thermal efficiency.
CombustorFuel burns. Hotter gas (>1500°C) means more work per kg of fuel.
TurbineExtracts power to drive the fan & compressor. Materials set the heat limit.
Bypass flowThe cool air going around the core — most of the thrust, quietly.
