AVA

A concept exploration of multi-signal sensor fusion — the idea that a defensive tracking system should weigh many imperfect signals together rather than trust any single one.

ADAPTIVE · VERIFIED · AWARENESS
⚠ CONCEPT SHOWCASE ONLY — This page presents a high-level vision. It contains no engineering, guidance, or targeting specifics, and describes no buildable system.

01 The problem

Systems that rely on a single kind of signal are easy to mislead. A lone sensor can be fooled by noise, reflections, or deliberate decoys — showing phantom contacts that look just as real as the genuine one.

AVA begins from a simple observation: one signal is a guess; many signals, cross-checked, become confidence.

The concept asks what it would look like to treat heat, sound, motion, and visual cues as independent votes — and to trust a contact only when those votes agree.

02 The idea: signal fusion

Instead of leaning on one input, the AVA concept imagines drawing on the full spectrum of available cues — then combining them so that no single misleading signal can carry a decision on its own.
