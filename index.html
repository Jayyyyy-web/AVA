AVA

A concept exploration of multi-signal sensor fusion for commercial aviation — the idea that situational awareness should weigh many signals together rather than trust any single instrument.

ADAPTIVE · VERIFIED · AWARENESS VIEW 3D MODEL
✈ CONCEPT SHOWCASE — This page presents a high-level vision for civilian flight awareness. It is illustrative only and describes no certified or buildable system.

01 The problem

A flight deck takes in many streams of information, and any single one can be misleading — a noisy return, a reflection, or a spurious alert can look just as real as the genuine picture. Leaning on one source invites mistakes.

AVA starts from a simple observation: one signal is a guess; many signals, cross-checked, become confidence.

The concept imagines treating traffic, weather, terrain, and visual cues as independent perspectives — and trusting the picture only where they agree, so uncertain returns fade into the background.

02 The idea: signal fusion

Rather than leaning on one input, the AVA concept imagines drawing on the full spread of available awareness cues — then combining them so no single misleading signal carries a decision on its own.
